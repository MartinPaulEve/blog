This page sets out whether and how automated and artificial intelligence (AI) tools are used across this website, eve.gd. It applies to the whole site: blog posts, essays, my biography, the publications list, and any other content published here.

## What is and is not written by AI

The written content on this site is human-written. I write my blog posts, essays, and biographical pages myself. These are not generated by AI tools.

There is one significant piece of automation on the site. My publications list is populated automatically from the Birkbeck Institutional Repository (BIROn) through an automated feed, rather than being typed out by hand. This is the main place where an automated process contributes to what you see.

Where I use AI assistance for anything else published on the site, I disclose it. If I have not disclosed AI involvement on a given page, that page was written by me.

## Scope, limitations, and safeguards

The specific tasks that may involve automation or AI are: retrieving and formatting my list of publications from BIROn, and, on occasion, drafting or editing assistance that is always disclosed where used. Everything else is human work.

I recognise that AI systems and automated feeds have real limitations. They can be inaccurate, produce plausible-sounding but wrong information, miss context, or misclassify items. I do not treat their output as automatically correct.

The main safeguard is human review before publication. Automated output is checked by me before it appears on the site. The publications feed in particular is reviewed before it goes live, so that errors in metadata, classification, or formatting can be caught and corrected.

## How AI-assisted content is marked

Where any content on this site has involved AI assistance, it is disclosed on the relevant page. I apply this convention consistently: the rule is simply that undisclosed content is my own, and anything that used AI assistance says so. This keeps the labelling predictable across the whole site.

## Oversight, review, and accountability

Automated and AI outputs on this site, including the auto-populated BIROn publications feed, are reviewed by a human before publication. That human is me, Martin Paul Eve.

The review process works like this: the automated feed proposes entries drawn from BIROn; I review those entries for accuracy and appropriateness; and only reviewed content is published. I am accountable for all content on this site, whether written by hand, assisted by AI, or drawn from an automated feed.

## How the publications feed is ordered and audited

The publications list is ordered by date, with the newest items shown first. Each entry's classification is auditable through the BIROn repository itself. In the list, a green download link indicates a green open-access accepted manuscript, and a gold link indicates a gold open-access version. Because the underlying records live in BIROn, the selection and the green-versus-gold classification of any item can be checked against the repository.
