<p>Following <a href="http://www.guardian.co.uk/books/booksblog/2011/may/13/writers-in-public-pynchon">the success</a> of last year's <a href="http://www.pynchoninpublic.com/">Pynchon in Public Day</a>, this May 8th will play host to further celebrations of perhaps the world's greatest living author: Thomas Pynchon.</p>
<img src="https://www.martineve.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/pipd.jpg" alt="Pynchon in Public Day" title="Pynchon in Public Day" style="width:750px;" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-2070" />
<p>I know that there are plans afoot in Munich and New York, but wanted to announce that a Brighton contingent will be meeting at 10am on Tuesday 8th of May at the Nia Cafe. Come join us to talk about Pynchon's works, literary projects in the online sphere or other matters pertaining to his writings.</p>
<p>Write by W.A.S.T.E., or email, with your photos and tweet on the #Pynchon2012 hashtag!</p>
