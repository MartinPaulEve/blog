<p>Today is the day that we've all been <s>dreading</s> waiting for. The final unveiling of the UK Higher Education White Paper.</p>
<p><img src="https://www.martineve.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/paper-1024x768.jpg" alt="Paper" title="Paper" style="width:750px;" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-1234" /></p>
<p>It's expected later today, but here's some early reactions.</p>
<p><a href="http://www.guardian.co.uk/education/2011/jun/28/naming-shaming-degrees-poor-jobs-record">The Guardian</a> runs with "'Naming and shaming' for degrees with poor jobs record", which gets to the core of the matter:</p>
<blockquote><p>Scrapping or overhauling "dead-end" courses would limit losses to the taxpayer from students who fail to repay their loans.</p></blockquote>
<p><a href="http://www.bbc.co.uk/news/education-13927710">The BBC</a> are going with "Universities shake-up eyes greater competition", focusing on a fight for proportion of places.</p>
<p>Aaron Porter has been taking the opportunity to point out some major flaws, stating:</p>
<blockquote><p>The #HEWhitePaper has failed to show how it will improve access to Uni or improve quality #missedopportunity</p></blockquote>
<p><a href="https://twitter.com/#!/renireni">@renireni</a> gives us this, which I think is absolutely spot on:</p>
<blockquote><p> Full implementation of the student as consumer. Cost of everything, value of nothing #hewhitepaper</p></blockquote>
<p>Meanwhile, the Tory minority on Twitter (<a href="https://twitter.com/#!/richardbrabner">@richardbrabner</a>) seem to have found a way of gamifying the concerns:</p>
<blockquote><p>Good morning <a href="https://twitter.com/#!/MarioCreatura">@MarioCreatura</a>, shall we try and find the most over-the-top reaction to the #HEWhitePaper today? Pint for the winner?</p></blockquote>
<p>Meanwhile, <a href="https://twitter.com/#!/timeshighered">@timeshighered</a> remains a great source for updates throughout the day. In the meanwhile, I'm going to use today to get away from all this arguing and actually go teach.</p>
