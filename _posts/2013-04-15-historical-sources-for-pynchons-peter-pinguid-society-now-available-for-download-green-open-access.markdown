<p>In a bid to ensure that as many of my publications are as open as possible, I've begun trawling the back catalogue and am pleased to say, after a conversation with the editor of <i>Pynchon Notes</i>, that I am free to post an accepted version of the article. The publication, which covers the <a href="https://www.martineve.com/2011/12/12/publication-historical-sources-for-pynchon%E2%80%99s-peter-pinguid-society-pynchon-notes-56-57-spring-fall-2009-pp-242-245/">historical sources for the Peter Pinguid society in <i>The Crying of Lot 49</i></a> was originally published in 2011. It will also shortly appear in the University of Lincoln's repository.</p>
