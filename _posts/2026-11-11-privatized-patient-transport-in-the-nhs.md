I am being discharged from hospital today after three months admission. But my transport home is now *over 4.5hrs late* and I am *not yet even allocated a vehicle*. I remain taking up a bed on the ward at the expense of the NHS. Meanwhile, this is what private outsourcing of transport does to the NHS:

Companies like G4S run private transport and, of course, optimize for profit, not patient welfare or NHS needs. So what they love is loading up a single truck with 5 dialysis patients all nearby in a nice circuit. Good fuel use for them. Single staff member handles 5x patients instead of 2x staff on a long-range trip with a single passenger like me.

There is even a chance now that this transfer home won't happen today. As we get into the afternoon, I have started looking up private transport costs to get home. This would be extremely expensive for me, given how far this hospital is from my home and the fact that I cannot use trains or public transport because I cannot walk and have no wheelchair with me. Oh, and I have three months' worth of luggage here, which I can't lift. Plus, a random Uber driver will probably not help me into a vehicle etc.

I have even heard of patients missing key procedures when they needed to be moved between hospitals, because these private corporations who run the transport are so bad. It is really not on.

But this is the kind of stealthy privatisation of the NHS that people don't notice until they need it. 

My elderly neighbours, for example, talk of how even Reform/Conservatives would never privatise the NHS. By this they mean that they believe even the most hardline governments would not remove free at the point of delivery. And in the tradition of ["leopards eating people's faces" discourse](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/leopards-eating-peoples-faces-party), they think somehow they would be exempted from insurance regimes that might cut them off from treatment. They probably would, tbh. Others would not be so lucky.

But they know nothing of this kind of privatisation where the outsourcing has costs for the NHS and makes for a totally miserable experience for patients. It really is outrageous.
