This post is the final in an [ongoing series](https://martineve.com/GreenPaper/) where I intend to develop my full _personal_ (_not_ institutional) response to the HE Green Paper. Comments are welcome to refine this.

You can now [download my full response in a Word document format](/images/Eve-Green-Paper-Response.doc). The document total is 18,750 words.

I hereby release this work under a [CC0 license](https://creativecommons.org/choose/zero/). You may reuse this without attribution, although I suggest that you do not submit it to BIS in precisely the same form but rather use it as the basis for your own response. I make no guarantee that this document will remain static until the consultation deadline. I hope this will encourage others to respond to the green paper.
