<p>While browsing the EThOS Thesis collection today, I suddenly became curious as to the history, statistically speaking, of doctorates in my field of literature: studies on Thomas Pynchon.</p>
<p>It emerges that, on record since 1978, there have been 23 doctorates awarded in the UK on, or in part concerning, the fiction of Thomas Pynchon.</p>
<p>Institutionally, there is a clear centre for UK Pynchon studies established at the University of Sussex, which has awarded two more doctorates on this topic as any other to date, the nearest rival being Nottingham. This is currently shifting, with Samuel Thomas founding a complementary (I won't say "rival"!) school in Durham where there are several PhDs in progress on this topic. Here's the chart of institutional totals:</p>

<p>Finally, this is just provided for curiosity value; it doesn't have merit as a decision for where to undertake a doctorate because people move from institution to institution. It could be that a supervisor at one university moved, and that subsequent doctorates where also his or her supervisee. Simply pick the place where you will get the best support and be able to do your work with the right people.</p>
