I want this website to be usable by as many people as possible, whatever technology they use and whatever their needs. This page explains how I try to achieve that, where the site currently falls short, and how you can get in touch if something does not work for you.

## What I aim for

I aim to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) at level AA. In practice, that means the site is built to:

- work with keyboard navigation, so you do not need a mouse;
- offer a light and dark theme toggle, so you can choose a comfortable contrast;
- allow you to resize the text in your browser without content breaking or overlapping;
- use semantic HTML, so screen readers and other assistive technologies can make sense of the structure.

This is my personal academic site, which I maintain in my own time, so I cannot promise the polish of a large institution. I do, though, take accessibility seriously and try to improve things steadily.

## Known limitations

I want to be honest about the areas where the site is not yet fully accessible:

- The blog archive goes back to around 2011. Some older posts contain images whose alternative text is missing or imperfect.
- Some linked PDFs, including my CV and copies of academic papers, may not be fully tagged, so they may not read well in a screen reader.
- Some older embedded media may not have captions or transcripts.

I am working through these as time allows, and newer content is written with accessibility in mind from the start.

## Fixing problems

I review the site's accessibility periodically, at least once a year. When someone reports a specific problem, I aim to address it within 30 working days. Where a fix is not straightforward, I will tell you what I plan to do and roughly when.

## Giving feedback

If you find anything on this site difficult or impossible to use, please tell me. Email me at [martin@eve.gd](mailto:martin@eve.gd) with the page address and a short description of the problem, and I will do my best to help. I aim to respond within 10 working days.

Your feedback genuinely helps, and I am grateful for it.
